Guide to Clinical Trials — Asia Pacific

My Role Senior Designer, publication layout and production
Art direction: Ryan Richard-Scro, Orange Square

Eighty pages. Thirteen countries. One coherent story.

PAREXEL, a global clinical research organization, needed an 80-page market guide covering clinical trial landscapes across the Asia Pacific region — a key sales and educational tool for use across 13 countries. The challenge was translating dense, technical content into layouts that were clear and accessible while working strictly within PAREXEL's established brand standards.

The art director at Orange Square established the visual direction and designed the opening pages. I joined the project and completed the remaining layouts, sourcing and selecting photography throughout and producing the publication to final file.
