Shaping Children's Products

My Role Creative Director, concept through production

Ten years. Major licensed partners. Thousands of pages brought to life.

At Publications International, I spent a decade shaping children's publishing across major licensed partnerships, including Disney, Mattel, Scholastic, and Nickelodeon, work that reached shelves at national mass-market retailers like Target, Walmart, Costco, and Babies"R"Us. The work went far beyond flat print to include pop-ups, lift-the-flaps, sound books, and sticker and activity formats, each with its own production logic. I worked hands-on with dielines and prepress files, coordinating closely with our embedded production team in China to ensure every novelty element met specification, while leading a team of designers from concept through final, shelf-ready product.
